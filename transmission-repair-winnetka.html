Symptoms that need transmission service

Common signs include not shifting, slipping in certain gears, and unusual noises, among other symptoms. Vehicles also come in with these problems:

  • Slipping gears — the engine revs but the car does not accelerate
  • Delayed or rough shifting between gears
  • A burning smell, or transmission fluid that looks dark or burnt
  • Grinding or shaking when changing gears
  • A check-engine light or transmission warning light
  • Red or brown fluid leaking under the vehicle
  • The car will not go into gear, or it stays in one gear

If you notice any of these, call (818) 882-5135. Diagnosis is free unless the transmission must be removed and opened. More symptom detail is on the transmission problems FAQ. Canoga Park drivers use this same shop. Directions from Canoga Park and nearby neighborhoods.

Our process

A transmission visit at the Winnetka shop follows four steps: diagnosis, a recommendation, the repair, and a road test.

  1. Diagnosis. Diagnosis is free as long as we don't have to remove and open up the transmission.
  2. Recommendation. It depends on how badly the transmission is damaged. We inspect it and recommend the right option: repair, rebuild, or replacement.
  3. Repair, rebuild, or replacement. We do the work we recommended. Cost depends on the vehicle and what the diagnosis finds. Call (818) 882-5135 for a free diagnosis and a written estimate before any work starts. A rebuild usually takes two days or more.
  4. Road test. After the repair, rebuild, or replacement, we road test the vehicle to confirm how it shifts.

Rebuild, replacement, or a used unit

A rebuild repairs the transmission already in the vehicle. Worn parts are replaced and the unit goes back in. Cost depends on the vehicle and what the diagnosis finds. Call (818) 882-5135 for a free diagnosis and a written estimate before any work starts. The job usually takes two days or more. Transmission work is covered by a 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor (whichever comes first).

A replacement installs a different transmission. For CVTs, we usually replace the unit rather than rebuild it.

A used transmission is a unit that came from another vehicle. Rebuild, replacement, or a used unit can each be the right choice. It depends on how badly the transmission is damaged. We inspect it and recommend the right option.

Automatic, manual, and CVT

We repair transmissions on almost every make and model. That includes automatic and manual transmissions. The right job — repair, rebuild, or replacement — depends on the diagnosis.

On a manual, the clutch is a separate part from the transmission. Clutch repair and replacement is its own service: clutch repair in Winnetka.

A CVT (continuously variable transmission) is different from a conventional automatic. For CVTs, we usually replace the unit rather than rebuild it.

When the fluid needs service and the transmission does not need to come apart, the visit is a transmission fluid service. A whine, clunk, or vibration can also come from the differential. On a truck or SUV, the transfer case is a separate repair from the transmission.
