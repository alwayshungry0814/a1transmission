Frequently Asked Questions

Does A1 Transmission repair Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Porsche, and Audi transmissions?

Yes. We work on almost all makes and models, including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Porsche, Audi, and VW automatic and manual transmissions, transfer cases, differentials, clutches, and brakes.

Why did the dealership quote a full replacement when my European car went into limp mode?

Dealerships often default to full assembly replacement even when the fault is an external conductor plate, seal/sleeve leak, sensor, or fluid issue. Bring it in for a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened) for an honest second opinion.

What warranty comes with European transmission repair?

All repair work is backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.
