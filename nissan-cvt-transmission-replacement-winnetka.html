Why Honest Diagnosis Comes First on Nissan CVTs

Not every Nissan Check Engine Light or sluggish shift means the CVT is dead. A failing speed sensor, low/degraded CVT fluid, or pressure sensor fault can mimic major transmission failure. We scan the TCM, inspect fluid condition, and road-test when safe—giving you a written explanation and estimate before any repair begins (CA BAR License #ARD 194825).

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I rebuild or replace a failing Nissan CVT transmission?

Nissan Jatco CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt because worn steel belts and scored pulley cones rarely hold up to a partial rebuild. See our full CVT Replacement guide.

What do Nissan CVT codes P17F0, P17F1, and P0846 mean?

P17F0 and P17F1 indicate CVT judder faults (slippage or valve body wear), while P0846 points to a fluid pressure sensor issue. Bring the car in for a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened) to verify the exact cause.

What warranty comes with a Nissan CVT replacement at A1 Transmission?

Every repair is backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.
