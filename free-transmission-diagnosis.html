What the free diagnosis is

The free diagnosis is the shop looking at the transmission before any repair, rebuild, or replacement. It is for drivers who notice a transmission problem and want to know what is wrong before they agree to work. The shop is at 20146 Saticoy Street B1, Winnetka, CA 91306, and it serves Winnetka, Canoga Park, and the San Fernando Valley.

Common warning signs are not shifting, slipping in certain gears, and noises. The shop works on almost all makes and models. CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt. More signs are on the transmission problems FAQ.

The one exception

Diagnosis is free unless the transmission must be removed and opened. If the transmission has to be removed and opened, that is not free. That is the only exception.

What happens after

The shop gives a written explanation of what it found. Rebuild versus replace depends on how badly the transmission is damaged. Cost depends on the vehicle and what the diagnosis finds. Call (818) 882-5135 for a free diagnosis and a written estimate before any work starts. A rebuild usually takes 2 days or more.
