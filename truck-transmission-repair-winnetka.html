Frequently Asked Questions

What truck transmissions does A1 Transmission rebuild and repair?

We service Ford, Chevy, GMC, Ram, Toyota, and Nissan pickup trucks, work vans, and 4x4 SUVs—including automatic rebuilds, manual clutches, transfer cases, differentials, and drive shafts.

Why is my Chevy Silverado, GMC Sierra, or Ford F-150 shuddering or shifting hard?

Shuddering at cruising speed or harsh shifts on 6-, 8-, and 10-speed truck automatics frequently stem from torque converter wear, fluid breakdown, or valve body/clutch issues. A free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened) identifies the exact fault.

How long does a truck transmission rebuild take?

A full transmission rebuild usually takes 2 days or more, depending on the truck and parts availability. Every job is backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor (whichever comes first).
