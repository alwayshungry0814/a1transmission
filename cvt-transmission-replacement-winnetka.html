Quick Answer

Should you rebuild or replace a CVT transmission? Unlike traditional geared automatics, continuously variable transmissions (CVTs) rely on precision steel belts or chains running on machined pulley cones. When internal wear occurs, CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt. First, bring your car to A1 Transmission in Winnetka for a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be removed and opened) to confirm whether the issue is a sensor, fluid problem, or internal CVT failure.

Why CVTs Are Usually Replaced Instead of Rebuilt

A traditional automatic transmission uses planetary gear sets, clutch packs, and bands that can be disassembled, inspected, and rebuilt with new wear items. A CVT uses two variable-diameter pulleys connected by a high-tension push belt or chain. Once the belt slips and scores the pulley surfaces, metal shavings circulate through the valve body and cooler.

Replacing the CVT assembly avoids repeat failures caused by scored pulleys or hidden debris, and gives you a clean baseline backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor (whichever comes first). Per California law, your written estimate and final invoice clearly identify whether a replacement unit is new, rebuilt, reconditioned, or used.

CVT vs. Traditional Automatic Transmission

Factor CVT Transmission Traditional Automatic
Internal design Pulleys + steel push belt or chain (no fixed gears) Planetary gear sets, clutch packs, bands, torque converter
Typical major repair Usually replaced rather than rebuilt Rebuilt or replaced depending on damage
Common symptoms Shuddering on takeoff, high RPM with weak acceleration, whining/droning, limp mode Slipping between gears, hard or delayed shifts, burnt fluid, gear-ratio codes
Fluid sensitivity Very sensitive — requires exact OEM-spec CVT fluid Requires correct ATF spec and periodic full fluid flush

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you rebuild or replace a failing CVT transmission?

CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt because worn belts, chains, and pulley cones rarely hold up to a partial bench rebuild. After a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened), we give you a written explanation and estimate before any work starts.

Which CVT vehicles does A1 Transmission work on?

We work on almost all makes and models with CVT transmissions, including Nissan, Honda, Subaru, Toyota, and Mitsubishi.

What are the warning signs of a failing CVT transmission?

Shuddering or jerking on acceleration, high engine RPM without matching speed, whining or droning noises, delayed engagement into Drive or Reverse, overheating warnings, or a Check Engine Light. See our full Transmission Warning Signs guide.

What warranty comes with a CVT replacement at A1 Transmission?

Repair work is backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.
