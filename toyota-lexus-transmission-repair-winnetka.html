High-Mileage Toyota & Lexus Maintenance Matters

Toyota and Lexus transmissions are built to last, which is why many San Fernando Valley drivers push them past 150,000 or 200,000 miles. Stop-and-go heat on the 101 and 118 freeways breaks down transmission fluid over time—even in "sealed" units without a dipstick. Catching degraded fluid, a leaking axle seal, or early torque converter shudder early can save you from a major overhaul.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Toyota and Lexus "lifetime" sealed transmissions still need fluid service?

Yes. Fluid breaks down with heat and mileage. Checking fluid condition and performing a full transmission fluid flush when appropriate helps prevent solenoid and torque converter wear.

Does A1 Transmission work on Toyota Tacoma, Tundra, and 4Runner 4x4 drivetrains?

Yes. We service the transmission, 4x4 transfer case, front and rear differentials, drive shafts, clutches, and brakes in one Winnetka shop.

What warranty comes with Toyota & Lexus transmission repair?

Every repair is backed by our 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.
