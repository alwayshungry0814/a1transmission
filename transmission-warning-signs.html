Transmission Warning Signs: Slipping, Leaks & Codes

Symptom Checklist for Winnetka & San Fernando Valley Drivers

Common transmission warning signs include not shifting, slipping in certain gears, whining or clunking noises, red fluid leaks, and Check Engine Light codes like P0700 or P0730. Quick Answer

What are the earliest signs of transmission trouble? Slipping in certain gears, hesitation or refusal to shift into Drive or Reverse, unusual whining, buzzing, or clunking noises, reddish-brown fluid under the car, or a Check Engine Light. Initial diagnosis at A1 Transmission in Winnetka is free unless the transmission must be removed and opened.

Transmission Symptom-to-Action Matrix

Symptom Common Causes Recommended Action
Slipping in certain gears (RPM rises, car doesn't accelerate) Low/degraded fluid, worn clutch packs, internal pressure loss, or CVT belt slip Avoid freeway driving; schedule a free initial diagnosis promptly
Not shifting / delayed engagement Shift solenoid, pressure sensor, low fluid, valve body, or internal clutch failure Stop driving if stuck in one gear or limp mode; call (818) 882-5135
Whining, clunking, or grinding noises Worn bearings, torque converter, planetary gears, differential, or low fluid Inspect soon before metal debris damages additional components
Red or dark brown fluid under the car Pan gasket, axle seal, cooler line, or front/rear transmission seal leak Do not run dry; small leaks are far cheaper to fix than a burned-up unit
Check Engine Light (P0700, P0730, P0741) TCM fault, gear ratio error, solenoid circuit, or torque converter clutch issue Get a proper diagnostic scan — not every code means a full rebuild

Common Transmission Trouble Codes Explained

  • P0700 (Transmission Control System Malfunction): Signals that the transmission computer has stored one or more specific fault codes that need a dedicated scan.
  • P0730 (Incorrect Gear Ratio): The transmission is slipping or failing to match expected input and output shaft speeds.
  • P0741 (Torque Converter Clutch Stuck Off): Often felt as shuddering at highway speeds or higher-than-normal RPMs while cruising.
  • P0750–P0775 (Shift Solenoid Codes): Electrical or hydraulic solenoid issues that can sometimes be resolved with a targeted repair rather than a full overhaul.
