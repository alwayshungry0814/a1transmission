Common Transmission Questions

Straight answers on diagnosis, turnaround time, warranty, and CVTs

Is transmission diagnosis really free at A1 Transmission?

Yes. Initial transmission diagnosis is free unless the transmission must be removed and opened to inspect internal damage. If removal and teardown are needed, we tell you up front before any work starts.

How long does a transmission rebuild take?

A full transmission rebuild usually takes 2 days or more, depending on the vehicle, internal damage found during diagnosis, and parts availability.

What warranty does A1 Transmission offer?

A1 Transmission backs repair work with a 1-year / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.

Do you rebuild or replace CVT transmissions?

CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt. After a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened), we give you a written explanation and estimate before any work starts. Read our CVT guide.
