Frequently Asked Questions

Does A1 Transmission repair both Honda automatic and CVT transmissions?

Yes. We work on Honda and Acura geared automatics, manual clutches, and CVTs. Geared automatics are repaired or rebuilt depending on internal damage, while failing CVTs are usually replaced rather than rebuilt.

Why is my Honda Odyssey, Pilot, or Acura MDX shuddering at 30 to 50 mph?

A rumble-strip vibration at cruising speeds on V6 Honda and Acura models is commonly tied to torque converter clutch shudder or degraded fluid. Bring it in for a free initial diagnosis (unless the unit must be opened) to pinpoint the cause.

How long does a Honda or Acura transmission rebuild take?

A full transmission rebuild usually takes 2 days or more, depending on the model and parts availability.
